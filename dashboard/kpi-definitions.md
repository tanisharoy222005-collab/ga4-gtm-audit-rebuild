# KPI Definitions

## Users

Unique website visitors.

Formula:

Users

---

## Sessions

Total website sessions.

Formula:

Sessions

---

## CTA Click Rate

Percentage of sessions resulting in a CTA click.

Formula:

CTA Clicks / Sessions

---

## Form Completion Rate

Percentage of form starts resulting in a lead.

Formula:

Generate Leads / Form Starts

---

## Conversion Rate

Percentage of sessions resulting in a lead.

Formula:

Generate Leads / Sessions

---

## Engagement Rate

Percentage of engaged sessions.

Formula:

Engaged Sessions / Sessions

---

## Average Events Per Session

Formula:

Total Events / Sessions
